Overview

Start with your first statement

Nothing is stored yet. This takes about two minutes, and nothing leaves this machine.

  1. Get the statement. Download a consolidated account statement (CAS) as a PDF from NSDL or CDSL. It lists your stocks, ETFs, bonds and mutual fund folios.
  2. Upload it on the Imports page with the PDF's password. NSDL and CDSL statements use your PAN in capitals. The file is read in memory and not stored.
  3. Check the result. The page shows whether each account adds up to the total printed on the statement. Anything that does not is listed under Data quality, with what to do.
