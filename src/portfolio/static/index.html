Portfolio

Upload a statement

Upload the CAS from NSDL or CDSL: it covers stocks, ETFs, bonds and mutual fund folios. A CAS from CAMS or KFintech is optional; it adds mutual fund transaction history. The PDF is read in memory and not stored. Neither is the password.

Totals

Downloads public end-of-day files (AMFI fund NAVs, NSE and BSE closing prices). Nothing about your holdings is sent.

Data completeness

    Value at each statement

    What you held on each statement date. It rises when you add money as well as when prices rise, so it is not your return. A hollow point means its stocks and funds come from statements more than a week apart.

    What changed between statements

    Compares your holdings on two statement dates. Each change in value is split into what came from holding more or less (bought or sold) and what came from price moves. This is worked out from statement values, not from your trades.
